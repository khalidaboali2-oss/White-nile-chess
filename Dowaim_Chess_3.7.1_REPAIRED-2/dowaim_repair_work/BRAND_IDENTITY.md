The platform brand is Dowaim Chess, while White Nile Chess Academy remains the parent institution and official identity.

Preferred display:

Do not remove the academy name from institutional metadata, ownership, documentation, or core backend identity. The public platform name can be changed back later without rebuilding the platform.

## Important
This is a branding/display change, not a data migration. Accounts, games, tournaments, ratings, teams, and backend functionality remain unchanged.
